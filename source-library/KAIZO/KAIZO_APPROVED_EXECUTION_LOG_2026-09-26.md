## 3. STAKEHOLDER VALUE MODEL — APPROVED DIRECTION
### Academy Owner
KAIZO should provide operational visibility into academy/team quality, athlete development, coach execution, KPIs, planning status, problems and reports. No evidence currently proves the complete production Owner layer.

### Technical Director
KAIZO should support season/mesocycle/microcycle/session planning, technical standards, athlete/team monitoring, coach quality, problem intelligence, competition preparation and decision support. Architecture supports the direction; production completeness remains open.

### Coach
KAIZO should support pre-session, during-session and post-session decisions: problem diagnosis, intervention selection, planning, drills, constraints, KPIs, retest and learning. This is the strongest currently evidenced user pathway, but full production integration remains open.

### Athlete
KAIZO should provide profile, needs/problems, training actions, measurable progress and next-step guidance while remaining subordinate to coach authority.

### Parent
KAIZO should provide understandable development follow-up, attendance/progress/strengths/areas to improve and appropriate support guidance; it must not replace the coach or expose inappropriate technical authority. Production Parent layer is not yet evidenced.
