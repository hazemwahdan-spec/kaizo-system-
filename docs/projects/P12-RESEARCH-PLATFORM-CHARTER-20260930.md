# P12 — Research Platform Charter

## Purpose
Governed research workspace above the frozen KAIZO Core.

## Scope
- Research project/case metadata
- Research question
- Evidence/source register with provenance and evidence status
- Observations and data notes
- Separate analysis/interpretation from hypothesis
- Methodology and limitations
- Research notes and follow-up
- Read-only summary/export of the current workspace state

## Governance
- No fabricated evidence or scientific-validity claim from data entry/completion alone.
- Observation, analysis, hypothesis, and decision are distinct fields.
- No autonomous coaching or athlete selection.
- No medical diagnosis or treatment.
- No predictive claims or normative ranking.
- No modification of frozen Core logic.
- P05 persistence, P13 RBAC, P14 minor/guardian consent, and P15 analytics remain separate gates.
- Research does not override Coach Final Authority.

## Runtime boundary
This phase is a static/browser-local research workspace. Local demo data is non-authoritative and is not a production research database.

## Acceptance
1. Create a research case.
2. Capture a research question.
3. Register evidence with provenance/status.
4. Record observation separately from interpretation/hypothesis.
5. Record methodology and limitations.
6. Run an independent static runtime check.
7. Commit closure evidence.
