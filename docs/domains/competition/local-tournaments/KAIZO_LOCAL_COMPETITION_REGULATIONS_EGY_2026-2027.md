## 3. Weigh-in — source page 2

- Weigh-in instructions are announced 72 hours before the tournament.
- The weighing list must match the previously submitted list and be signed/stamped as specified in the source.
- The athlete must present the renewed Federation card; the document says electronic images of the card are not accepted.
- Belt grade is evidenced by the Federation card or the original certificate issued by the Egyptian Judo Federation.
- A committee appointed by the Higher Referees Committee oversees weighing; the responsible officials sign the weigh-in sheets.
- Each athlete is weighed once. The source states that a mismatch between the registered and measured weight results in removal from the list/draw.
- The source permits a coach to request a correction during the trial weighing, with the correction recorded in the notes before the sheet is handed to the weighing referee. It says there is no later increase/decrease after that handover.
- The source states a special allowance for female athletes wearing a judogi: up to 0.5 kg above the official weight; it also states an allowance of 200 g for male athletes wearing swimming trunks. **These allowances must be reconfirmed with the event program and officials before use.**
- Random weighing is held on the morning before competition starts and opens one hour before the start of competition. Athletes must bring their Federation card. The source says an athlete's random weight must not exceed the maximum official weight for the category by more than 5%.
- The source gives the organizing committee discretion to apply random weighing in preliminary rounds for Buds and Flowers.

## 6. Competition formats — source page 3

The document lists four individual competition-management systems:

1. **System 1:** Athletes in one weight category are divided into groups of four; round-robin competition is held within each group to determine first and second place.
2. **System 2:** Athletes in one weight category are divided into groups of eight; group matches use a knockout system with repechage for the first- and second-place outcomes, as described in the source.
3. **System 3:** Direct knockout with repechage from the round of 16.
4. **System 4:** Direct knockout with repechage from the round of 8.

The source states that the Federation's technical and competitions committees may introduce a special format for some age groups to suit participation numbers and competition development; such changes are to be announced in the tournament program. The applicable system is therefore **event-specific**, not a fixed KAIZO default.
