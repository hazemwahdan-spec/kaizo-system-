## Purpose
This pack records the external research used to strengthen and contextualize the already-closed REF-016 Digital Twin Architecture. It does not reopen or replace the approved KAIZO architecture.

## Architecture Implications for KAIZO
Real-world athlete/training process
→ Data/Observation
→ Twin State + Context
→ Knowledge / Evidence
→ Decision Engine + AI Services
→ Human Authority / Safety-HOLD
→ Intervention
→ Athlete Response
→ KPI / Retest
→ Twin State Update

Digital Twin is therefore the dynamic state/context representation, not the LLM, AI agent, database, dashboard, knowledge graph, or decision engine by itself.

## Minimum Defensible KAIZO Twin
1. Real-world referent.
2. Digital representation.
3. State and temporal history.
4. Context and constraints.
5. Synchronization from observations/events.
6. Knowledge/evidence linkage.
7. Decision connection.
8. Feedback from intervention and response.
9. Retest/state update.
10. Human authority, safety/HOLD, and auditability.

AI, autonomous agents, and full simulation are not mandatory elements of the minimum defensible architecture.
