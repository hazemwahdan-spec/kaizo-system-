# KAIZO Phase 7 — Product Navigation / Surface Contract

## Purpose
Define the four commercial product surfaces without changing Core Engine authority.

## Roles
- Academy: operational oversight within academy-owned scope.
- Coach: primary decision surface and final human authority.
- Athlete: self progress and approved feedback only.
- Parent: explicitly linked athlete progress and approved reports only.

## Governance
Navigation is not authorization. Client-side hiding is insufficient; server-side identity and RBAC enforcement is required.
No product surface may authorize execution, override safety, bypass evidence gates, change a coach decision, or export unapproved data.

## Next Gate
Identity/RBAC integration boundary, followed by tenancy isolation and runtime acceptance evidence.
